# Business findings

The historical accepted portfolio includes 2,260,668 valid loans and $34.004bn funded principal. The funding-weighted origination interest rate is 13.38%. Observed outstanding principal totals $9.510bn; it is a snapshot exposure, not expected credit loss.

There are 1,348,059 resolved loans and 269,320 charged-off loans. Charged-off / resolved is 19.98%, with only 59.63% of the total portfolio resolved. Current loans remain outside that denominator. Do not call this a lifetime default rate or a model accuracy score.

Grade A shows 6.04% charged-off among resolved loans versus 49.67% in grade G. Compare sample sizes, vintage mix and resolved coverage before attributing differences to grade. The report includes Wilson confidence bounds and maturity coverage for this reason.

Funding and outstanding exposure concentrate in grades B and C. These observations justify further concentration and vintage review; they do not prove that changing approvals would improve returns. Borrower income and DTI comparisons are descriptive and require confounding and fairness review before any policy use.
